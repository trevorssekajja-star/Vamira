Our Story

Born in Kampala.
Crafted for the World.

Vamira Collections is Kampala's premier destination for authentic, imported fragrances. Nestled in the heart of the city at the Vienna Building, we were founded on a singular belief: that every Ugandan deserves access to world-class luxury without compromise.

We curate only the finest men's, women's, and unisex fragrances from the most celebrated perfume houses around the world — each bottle a story, each spritz an experience. From the warm, spiced oud notes favoured by the discerning gentleman, to the delicate florals that define modern femininity, our collection speaks to every identity and occasion.

At Vamira, we don't just sell perfume — we deliver confidence, elegance, and the unmistakable power of a signature scent.
